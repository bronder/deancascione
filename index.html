Biography

American Metal, Melodic Heart

Dean Cascione is an American Metal guitarist and an innovative force in today's instrumental rock scene. Committed to shattering shred stereotypes, he delivers melodic music, charged with passion and contrast.

A showcase of Neoclassical fretboard pyrotechnics… channels the spirit of Rhoads and the best of Malmsteen. — Press

Praised as “dangerous with a guitar” and “a bright star on the international guitar scene,” Dean has released multiple acclaimed albums and collaborated with legends in the metal world. His debut Guitar Chop Shop topped charts in 2008, and he's continued to push boundaries with releases like Neoclassical Fire (2011) and Shred Crusade (2015).

In 2010, Dean recorded with MANOWAR on the worldwide release MAGIC: A Tribute to Ronnie James Dio. Drawing inspiration from Cacophony & Racer X, he joined forces with “Guitar Maxx” Maxxwell Carlisle to release several singles. Dean has performed at festivals, clinics, and shows across the country.
